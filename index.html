About the model

The capillary is divided into 400 segments. In each, filtration = (Kf/400) × local NFP. Protein-free fluid leaving the capillary raises protein concentration downstream, and πGC is recalculated with the Landis–Pappenheimer equation (π = 2.1C + 0.16C² + 0.009C³, C in g/dL). Values are whole-kidney (both kidneys): normal Kf 12.5 mL/min/mmHg, RPF about 650 mL/min.

In arteriole mode, PGC is set by where it sits between the afferent and efferent resistances (downstream pressure about 13 mmHg). Plasma flow is (MAP − 13) divided by total resistance. Autoregulation adjusts afferent tone to hold flow roughly constant between MAP ≈ 75 and 180 mmHg. Tubuloglomerular feedback and hormonal responses are not modeled, so real kidneys defend GFR better at low MAP than this model does. This is a teaching model, not a clinical calculator.
